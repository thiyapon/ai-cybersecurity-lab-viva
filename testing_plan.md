# Student Testing and Before/After Analysis

Run at least 10 real student sessions. Obtain consent, avoid collecting names unless necessary, and use participant IDs.

Suggested flow:
1. Give a short pre-test.
2. Let the student use the study guide and viva practice.
3. Give a comparable post-test.
4. Record time taken, confidence rating and feedback.
5. Compare results and document limitations.

CSV columns suggested: participant_id, topic, pre_score, post_score, pre_minutes, post_minutes, confidence_before_1_to_5, confidence_after_1_to_5, comments.

Score improvement = post_score - pre_score.
Percentage improvement = (post_score - pre_score) / pre_score * 100, only if pre_score > 0.

Do not fabricate data. A 10-session pilot is small and cannot prove broad effectiveness or causation.
